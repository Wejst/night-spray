Night Spray VR

A Prague-style street, a tram depot and a pocket full of cans. Clean trams in the day for cash, buy paint at the trafika, and paint at night.

Checking for a VR headset…

Quest controls

  • Left stick: walk. Hold left grip: run
  • Right stick: turn
  • Right trigger: spray, mop or press a button
  • A: switch spray can / mop
  • B: brush size (thin, medium, fat)
  • Y: open or close the palette on your left hand

PC controls

  • WASD walk, Shift run, mouse to look
  • Left click: spray, mop or press a shop button
  • Q can / mop, R brush size
  • 1–8 pick color, Esc free the mouse

How it works

  • Day 15 min: mop the tags off the tram at the depot, $2 per clean patch
  • Night 10 min: fewer people, best time to paint
  • Trafika (kiosk): cans $15, water $3, food $5
  • Running makes you thirsty, walking makes you hungry. Hit zero and surgery costs $200 and all your paint
  • Cleaners buff 25% of pieces each morning
